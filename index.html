TTRPGMap Support

Get help with maps, file imports and purchases.

Email TTRPGMap support or visit the Telegram community.

Report a problem

Include your device model, operating system, TTRPGMap version and the steps that reproduce the issue. Send a screenshot or map only if you want to share it. Keep payment details and sensitive information out of the report.

Restore a purchase

Open Workshop in TTRPGMap and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

For billing or refund requests, contact Apple.

Manage your documents

Open or delete maps from their saved location in Files on iPhone or iPad, or Finder on Mac.
