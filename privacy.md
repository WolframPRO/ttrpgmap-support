# TTRPGMap Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

## Maps and documents

TTRPGMap does not require a TTRPGMap account. It processes maps and related files on your device. You choose where to save, import, or export documents. If you choose iCloud Drive, another storage provider, or a sharing destination, that service handles the files you send under its own privacy terms. TTRPGMap does not automatically send your map files to the developer.

## In-app purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. TTRPGMap uses Apple's StoreKit to check and restore access to purchased features and content. The developer does not receive your card details through the app.

## Analytics and advertising

TTRPGMap does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## Support

If you contact support by email or Telegram, we use the information you choose to send to investigate and respond to your request. Telegram and your email provider handle messages under their own privacy terms. You can request access to or deletion of support information you have shared by emailing [TTRPGMap support](mailto:mister.vladika@gmail.com).

You can manage or delete saved maps using the app or the file location where you stored them.
