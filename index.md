# TTRPGMap Support

For help with maps, file imports, or in-app purchases, email [TTRPGMap support](mailto:mister.vladika@gmail.com) or visit the [Telegram community](https://t.me/dnd_master_community).

When reporting a problem, include your device model, OS version, TTRPGMap version, and the steps that led to the problem. Share a map or screenshot only if you are comfortable doing so. Please keep payment details and other sensitive information out of your report.

If you need to restore a purchase, open the in-app store and use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles payment and refund requests.
