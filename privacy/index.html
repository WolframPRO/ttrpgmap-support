Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026

Maps and documents

TTRPGMap does not require a TTRPGMap account. It processes maps and related files on your device. You choose where to save, import or export documents. If you use iCloud Drive, another storage provider or a sharing destination, that service handles files under its own terms. TTRPGMap does not automatically send your maps to the developer.

In-app purchases

Apple processes purchases. TTRPGMap uses Apple’s StoreKit to check and restore access to purchased features. The developer does not receive your card details through the app.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not include advertising or analytics code. GitHub describes its handling of visitor data in its privacy statement.

Support requests

If you contact us by email or Telegram, we use the information you choose to send to investigate and respond. Your email provider or Telegram handles messages under its own privacy terms.

You can request access to or deletion of information you shared with support by emailing TTRPGMap support. You can delete maps from their saved location on your device or in the storage service you chose.
